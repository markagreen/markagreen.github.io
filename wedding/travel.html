GETTING THERE

Travel

Everything you need to make your journey as easy as possible.

BY CAR

Driving

There are two public car parks at the venue, but we cannot reserve spaces so availability is not guaranteed. One can also park on street on Calderstones Road or Harthill Road close by.

BY TRAIN

Train

Liverpool Lime Street station is the main station for the city, although Liverpool South Parkway is closer to the venue.

BY TAXI

Taxis

Liverpool has Uber with good availability. We also recommend using Alpha taxis which can be booked in advance. Taxis can drop off/pick up close to the entrance to avoid walking through the park.

BY BUS

Parking

From the city centre the 75 and 86C buses drop closest at Menlove/Cromptons Lane junction, with a 7 min walk to the venue. The 86 bus runs from the city centre or South Parkway station, with a 20 min walk via the stop in Allerton Road.
