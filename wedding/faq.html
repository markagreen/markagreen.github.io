FAQ

The things you probably want to know.

What should I wear?

Formal wear. It may be warm so please dress appropriately for the weather.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive from 1pm in good time for a prompt start at 2pm.

Can I bring a plus one?

Unfortunately we are limited on space, so we're only able to accommodate those named on the invitation.

Are children invited?

We are keeping the wedding adults-only, but dependent children are of course welcome.

Will there be food for dietary requirements?

Yes. Please include any dietary requirements or allergies when you RSVP.

Should I bring a gift?

Honestly, your presence is more than enough. Please don't feel you need to bring a gift.

Is the venue accessible?

The venue is fully accessible with step-free access. If you have a specific requirement, please do get in contact with us and we will help accommodate.

Is there parking?

There are two public car parks at the venue, but we cannot guarantee any availability. On-road partking is available on Calderstones Road.

Can I take photos?

Yes, please do! Feel free to send us your favourites afterwards. There will also be a photographer on the day capturing our special moments.
